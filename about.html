About SILENTIUM

SILENTIUM is a solo-narrator horror fiction podcast following Vatican linguist Father Tommaso Lanza as he investigates deaths for the Custodia Ultimae Vocis, a secret Church institution studying an impossible language that manifests at the moment of death.

Each episode documents a new case as Father Tommaso attempts to translate the Tongue, an ancient language that has been appearing in dying people's mouths for over 2,000 years. Is this language the key to finally understand God's design?

If you came here from Reddit, or you're looking for something in the vein of The Magnus Archives, Archive 81, or Old Gods of Appalachia — SILENTIUM lives in that same tradition of first-person, case-file horror fiction, just filtered through Vatican linguistics and cosmic dread instead of a cursed archive.

Content Warnings

  • Death and dying
  • Body horror
  • Religious themes and theological questioning
  • Existential dread
  • Descriptions of physical transformation
